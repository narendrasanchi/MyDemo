About Amazon Polly

Amazon Polly is a cloud service that converts text into lifelike speech. It offers dozens of voices across a variety of languages and includes both standard and neural text-to-speech.

  • Multiple Voices: Choose from male and female voices
  • Neural TTS: More natural and human-like speech
  • SSML Support: Speech Synthesis Markup Language for fine control
  • Multiple Formats: MP3, OGG, and PCM audio formats
